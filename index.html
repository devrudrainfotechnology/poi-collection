Complete Instructions

Getting Started:

  1. Choose Map Type: Select OpenStreetMap or Satellite view
  2. Upload KML (Optional): Click "Upload KML" to load existing POI markers
  3. Navigate Map: Use mouse wheel to zoom, drag to pan
  4. Set Building Location: Click on the building (Blue marker appears)
  5. Set Display Location: Click on the sign/display (Red marker appears)
  6. Street View: Automatically updates to show location
  7. Add Categories: Type new category and click "Add"
  8. Fill Form: Enter POI details
  9. Save: Click "Save POI"
  10. Export: Click "Export Excel" to download data

Checking Satellite Image Date:

  • For accurate dates, use Google Earth Pro (free desktop app)
  • In Google Earth: View → Historical Imagery
  • Slider shows available image dates
  • Look for images after May 2023
